About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Information Technology Una. Previously, I worked as a Postdoctoral Fellow at Chennai Mathematical Institute.

My research interests include Algorithms, Computational Algebra, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Parameterized Complexity.
